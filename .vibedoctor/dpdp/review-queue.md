# DPDP Review Queue

This queue lists unresolved organisational, legal, and product questions.
It is part of a **DPDP technical readiness** assessment — not legal certification.

Do not re-ask questions already answered by deterministic evidence in the control matrix.

## Developer

- **DPDP-REQ-001** (PARTIAL, low): How is Data Principal identity verified for rights requests?

## Product

- **DPDP-CHILD-001** (PARTIAL, high): Does the service knowingly process children's personal data?
- **DPDP-CONSENT-001** (PARTIAL, medium): Is consent free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous?
- **DPDP-CONSENT-002** (PARTIAL, low): Who is the system of record for consent proofs?

- **DPDP-WITHDRAW-001** (PARTIAL, medium): Can a Data Principal withdraw consent as easily as they gave it?
